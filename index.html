01 / Forecasting · RetailDemo available

FORESIGHT

Turn weekly retail demand into forecasts, stockout signals, and practical inventory decisions.

  • Python
  • Time series
  • Streamlit
Open demo Source
Read case study +

The question

How can a retail planner anticipate demand and identify inventory risk?

The approach

Clean 73,100 retail records, aggregate 100 Store × Product series, and evaluate weekly forecasts with rolling-origin validation against a seasonal-naive baseline.

The deliverable

A planning dashboard, transparent stockout and overstock scores, and a FastAPI scoring service.

Evidence & context

Holdout WAPE: 24.4%, compared with 33.6% for the baseline. These are dataset evaluation results from a simulated NorthBay Living engagement, not measured client impact.
